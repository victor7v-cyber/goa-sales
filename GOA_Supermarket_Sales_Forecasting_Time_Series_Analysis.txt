    "## 4. Promotion analysis — quantified result\n",
    "\n",
    "Compared average sales on promotional versus non-promotional days. The calculation below produces the exact percentage difference directly from the underlying dataset, rather than relying on visual estimates from the chart.\n",
    "\n",
    "**Interpretation:** A positive percentage means average sales were higher on promotional days; it does **not** by itself establish that promotions caused the increase.\n"
   ],
   "id": "fa6ff2f1-2da4-4ecf-98d5-254f82db23d7"
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "code",
   "execution_count": null,
   "metadata": {},
   "outputs": [],
   "source": [
    "promo_sales = df.groupby(\"promotion\")[\"sales\"].agg([\"mean\", \"count\"]).rename(columns={\"mean\": \"average_sales\", \"count\": \"days\"})\n",
    "\n",
    "# Assumes promotion=0 means no promotion and promotion=1 means promotion.\n",
    "no_promo_avg = promo_sales.loc[0, \"average_sales\"]\n",
    "promo_avg = promo_sales.loc[1, \"average_sales\"]\n",
    "absolute_difference = promo_avg - no_promo_avg\n",
    "percentage_difference = (absolute_difference / no_promo_avg) * 100\n",
    "\n",
    "print(\"Promotion comparison\")\n",
    "print(promo_sales)\n",
    "print(f\"\\nAverage sales — No promotion: {no_promo_avg:,.2f}\")\n",
    "print(f\"Average sales — Promotion: {promo_avg:,.2f}\")\n",
    "print(f\"Difference: {absolute_difference:,.2f}\")\n",
    "print(f\"Percentage difference: {percentage_difference:.2f}%\")\n",
    "\n",
    "plt.figure(figsize=(7, 4))\n",
    "sns.barplot(x=[\"No Promotion\", \"Promotion\"], y=[no_promo_avg, promo_avg])\n",
    "plt.ylabel(\"Average Sales\")\n",
    "plt.title(\"GOA Supermarket — Average Sales: Promotion vs. No Promotion\")\n",
    "plt.tight_layout()\n",
    "plt.show()\n"
